# Contributing

This archive aims to be the most complete, accurate public record of the UCI Beall Butterworth Competition. Corrections, additions, and source links are welcome from anyone - alumni, current students, faculty, judges, mentors, family members of past competitors, or curious observers.

## Ways to contribute

1. **Open an issue** if you spot an error, a missing team member, an undocumented year, or a broken source link.
2. **Open a pull request** with the corrected fact and a citation. Small PRs (one fact at a time) are easier to review than sweeping rewrites.
3. **Fill in the blanks.** Many cells are marked `Not Listed`, `Undisclosed`, or `*No public record found*`. If you have first-hand knowledge or a source, please replace those sentinels.
4. **Add a missing year.** The competition ran before 2012 (the inaugural Butterworth was around 2003-2004), but pre-2012 winners are not currently online. If you have a program booklet, press release, or photo from those years, please share.
5. **"Where are they now?" updates.** Did your team incorporate? Raise funding? Pivot? Shut down? Become acquired? Add a footnote to your year file with a source link.

## What to include in a correction

- The year and award category you are changing.
- The new value (team name, prize amount, member name, project description, etc.).
- A source link - ideally a primary one (see ranking below).

## Source quality (in order of preference)

1. Official BBComp pages on `bbcomp.tech.uci.edu`, including the per-year `past/...-winners/` and `past/<year>-participants-and-results/` URLs.
2. UCI press releases on `news.uci.edu`, `engineering.uci.edu`, `ics.uci.edu`, `innovation.uci.edu`, `merage.uci.edu`, `chancellor.uci.edu`, or `grad.uci.edu`.
3. Reputable news coverage (Orange County Business Journal, Philanthropy News Digest, etc.).
4. The team's own company website or product page.
5. A founder's LinkedIn or first-person account.

Wikipedia and AI-generated summaries are not acceptable as primary sources, but they can point you to the original.

## Style

- Cross-reference shared placements as `(See <Other Row>)` rather than duplicating member lists.
- Prize amounts are written as `$10,000`, no decimals, comma thousands separator.
- Member lists are comma-separated `First Last, First Last, ...` in the order the source presented them.
- Do not inflation-adjust historical prize amounts - record the dollar figure as it was awarded that year.
- When a fact cannot be verified from a public source, mark it `UNVERIFIED` and leave a note in the file's `## Notes` section so the next reader knows to keep looking.

## Code of conduct

Be kind and accurate. This is a record of student work - including the work of people who are still early in their careers - so prefer additions to deletions, and prefer dispassionate factual edits to commentary.
