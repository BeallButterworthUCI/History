# Faculty Mentor Profiles

[← Back to main archive](../README.md)

A handful of UCI faculty members have mentored multiple BBComp teams - some across both eras. This document profiles the recurring faculty mentors who shaped winning entries.

## Prof. Michelle Khine (Biomedical Engineering)

- **Position:** Professor in the Henry Samueli School of Engineering, UCI Biomedical Engineering. Affiliated with the Beckman Laser Institute. Founding Director of **Faculty Innovation** at the Samueli School of Engineering and founding Director of **BioENGINE** (BioEngineering Innovation and Entrepreneurship) at UCI. UCI Associate Dean of Undergraduate Education (since 2021).
- **Notable work:** "Shrinky Dink microfluidics" - a method for printing microfluidic patterns onto Shrinky Dink polymer sheets with a laser-jet printer, then heating them in a toaster oven to create micro-channels and microwells. Goes from concept to working device in minutes. Pandemic work used Shrinky-Dink electrodes for SARS-CoV-2 spike-protein saliva detection.
- **Companies:** **Scientific Founder of 6 startup companies**, including:
  - **Shrink Nanotechnologies Inc.** (the first start-up ever spun out of UC Merced, predating her UCI tenure)
  - **Fluxion Biosciences** (San Francisco; spun from her graduate-school dissertation work on single-cell electroporation)
  - **Vena Vitals** (Y Combinator-backed; co-founded 2019 with Joshua Kim and Ray Liu; commercialized the Qardiac BBComp 2019 winner technology)
  - Also serves as Advisory Board Member at **Novoheart**.
- **Honors:** 2009 MIT Technology Review TR35 Award (Top 35 Innovators Under 35 in the world); 2011 Marie Claire Magazine Women on Top Awards.
- **BBComp mentees:**
  - 2014 Beall 1st - **Spero Diagnostics** (rapid sepsis diagnostic, with Prof. Zoran Nenadic)
  - 2014 Beall 2nd - **A Hundred Tiny Hands** (children's Inventor's Toolboxes; now active nonprofit at 100tinyhands.org)
- **BBComp founder co-foundings:**
  - **Vena Vitals** (2019 Beall 1st Qardiac team Joshua Kim) - this is the only confirmed instance of a BBComp faculty mentor going on to co-found a venture-backed company with a BBComp competitor.
- **Sources:** [engineering.uci.edu profile](https://engineering.uci.edu/users/michelle-khine), [Khine Lab Group site](https://shrink.eng.uci.edu/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelle_Khine), [Novoheart advisory board](https://www.novoheart.com/michelle-khine-phd).

## Prof. Don Patterson (Informatics)

- **Position:** Professor in the Informatics Department, Donald Bren School of ICS.
- **Research areas:** ubiquitous computing, human-computer interaction, AI, context-aware computing, sensors / GPS / RFID / accelerometers, gesture recognition, cloud computing. Notable contributions to "collapse informatics" - the intersection of HCI with sustainability and ICT4D.
- **Honors:** UBICOMP 10-Year Impact Award; AIJ 5-Year Impact Award; CHI 2012 Best Paper Award (for "Collapse Informatics: Augmenting the Sustainability & ICT4D Discourse in HCI"); 2014 Outstanding Professor Award for the Donald Bren School of ICS.
- **BBComp mentees:**
  - 2012 Butterworth Finalist - **Rims** (RFID Inventory Management System)
  - 2012 Butterworth Finalist - **Whobot** (social shopping)
- **Sources:** [UCI faculty profile](https://faculty.uci.edu/profile/?facultyId=5316), [djp3.net](https://www.djp3.net/).

## Prof. Ramesh Jain (Computer Science, Bren Chair)

- **Position:** Distinguished Professor Emeritus in the Department of Computer Science, Donald Bren School of ICS. Founding Director of the **UCI Institute for Future Health**.
- **Distinction:** in 2005 named the **first Bren Professor in Information and Computer Science** for the Donald Bren School - the named-chair role that anchors the school's faculty leadership.
- **Research areas:** computer vision, AI, multimedia computing, experiential computing, digital health. Sometimes called the "Father of Multimedia."
- **Honors:** Fellow of ACM, IEEE, AAAS, IAPR, AAAI, and SPIE. ACM SIGMM Technical Achievement Award 2010.
- **BBComp mentees:**
  - 2012 Butterworth Finalist - **Pictorria** (web-based image-understanding service)
  - 2013 Butterworth Finalist - **Dentiverse** (medical practice manager)
- **Sources:** [ICS profile](https://ics.uci.edu/people/ramesh-jain/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramesh_Jain).
