# Glossary

[← Back to main archive](../README.md)

Terms used throughout this archive, defined for readers who are new to UCI or to the BBComp ecosystem.

## The Competition

**BBComp** - the UCI Beall Butterworth Competition. Common shorthand for the joint event.

**Butterworth Product Development Competition** - the software / ICS track of BBComp. Hosted by the Donald Bren School of Information and Computer Sciences. Originally a standalone competition starting around 2003-2004.

**Beall Student Design Competition** - the hardware / engineering track of BBComp. Hosted by the Henry Samueli School of Engineering. Launched in 2014.

**International Collaboration Prize** - the third BBComp track since 2025 (called the **Brazilian Collaboration Prize** in 2023 and 2024). Awarded for joint UCI / PUCRS teams.

**PUCRS** - Pontifical Catholic University of Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil. UCI's partner university for the International Collaboration Prize since 2023.

**Demo Day** - the final round of BBComp, held in May. Teams present their products to a panel of industry judges, who select 1st / 2nd / 3rd in each track.

**Coaching session** - one of three or four small-group meetings between Demo Day applicants and BBComp's industry advisor pool, held in April and May.

**Workshop** - one of six early-cycle (January-March) sessions where all entered teams learn product-development concepts (pretotyping, user-centered design, IP protection, customer interviewing, etc.).

## UCI Schools and Centers

**Donald Bren School of Information and Computer Sciences (Bren School / ICS)** - UCI's school for computer science, informatics, and statistics. Hosts the Butterworth track. Named for real-estate developer Donald Bren after a 2003 naming gift.

**Henry Samueli School of Engineering (Samueli)** - UCI's engineering school. Hosts the Beall track. Named for Broadcom co-founder Henry Samueli.

**Paul Merage School of Business (Merage)** - UCI's business school. Hosts the Beall Center for Innovation and Entrepreneurship and the Stella Zhang New Venture Competition.

**Claire Trevor School of the Arts** - hosts the Beall Center for Art + Technology (a separate entity from the Beall Center for Innovation and Entrepreneurship).

**UCI Beall Applied Innovation** - UCI's central innovation hub at 5270 California Ave. Renamed from "UCI Applied Innovation" in 2019 after a multimillion-dollar Beall Family gift. Houses The Cove and Wayfinder.

**The Cove** - co-working space inside UCI Beall Applied Innovation. Has hosted multiple BBComp Demo Day & Awards ceremonies.

**ANTrepreneur Center** - UCI's undergraduate-facing entrepreneurship program. Founded 2014. Funded in part by Blackstone Charitable Foundation.

**Wayfinder** - a no-cost incubator for UC-affiliated startups, run by UCI Beall Applied Innovation. Several BBComp winners (e.g., Grasshopperfund) have entered Wayfinder after their cycle.

**Beall Center for Innovation and Entrepreneurship** - the Merage-school entrepreneurship center. Founded 2007 with a $5M gift from the Beall Family Foundation. Runs the Stella Zhang NVC.

**Stella Zhang New Venture Competition (NVC)** - UCI's flagship business-plan competition, sister to BBComp. >$100K prize pool, five tracks (Business Products & Services, Consumer Products, Consumer Services, Life Sciences, Social Enterprise). The companion archive at [StellaZhangUCI/History](https://github.com/StellaZhangUCI/History) covers it in the same format used here for BBComp.

**Blackstone LaunchPad at UCI** - campus-wide startup mentorship program funded by the Blackstone Charitable Foundation. Several BBComp founders (Lily McGrale of forMED, Caitlyn Yang of Grasshopperfund) have been Blackstone fellows.

## People

**Paul Butterworth** - UCI alum (B.S. CS '74, M.S. CS '81), enterprise-software executive (Forte / Sun / Oracle / AmberPoint / VANTIQ), and the founding sponsor of the Butterworth Product Development Competition since approximately 2003-2004.

**Jo Butterworth** - UCI alum (B.A. Social Sciences '75) and Paul's wife. Co-leads Butterworth philanthropy at UCI.

**Donald R. Beall** - retired Chairman and CEO of Rockwell International. Founding sponsor of the Beall Student Design Competition since 2014. Beall Family philanthropy at UCI totals near $30M as of 2019.

**David Ochi** - adjunct professor in the Donald Bren School of ICS and Paul Merage School of Business. Director of the Beall Butterworth Competition since the inaugural joint edition in 2014. Previously at the UCI ANTrepreneur Center and Blackstone LaunchPad at UCI.

**Rafael Prikladnicki** - PUCRS associate professor and UCI visiting scholar. Facilitated the launch of the UCI / PUCRS partnership in 2023.

- **\*No public record found\*** - we searched multiple sources and did not find the data.
- **UNVERIFIED** - the value comes from a single secondary source and has not been corroborated against a primary source.

## Acronyms

**BLE** - Bluetooth Low Energy. The wireless standard underlying both 2014's Sparky / Blueberry and 2016's Tag Team (a crowd-GPS Bluetooth tag five years before Apple's AirTag).

**BME** - Biomedical Engineering. Department within the Henry Samueli School. Many Beall winners are BME senior-design teams.

**EEG** - Electroencephalogram. Brain-wave monitoring. Featured in 2019's CenSyn (now CenSyn Inc., commercial), 2019's CenSyn precursor research from Trevor Silence et al, and 2023's Encephalopod.

**FDA 510(k)** - the U.S. Food and Drug Administration's premarket-notification clearance pathway for medical devices. CenSyn Inc. is currently pursuing 510(k) clearance.

**ICS** - the Donald Bren School of Information and Computer Sciences (the abbreviation predates the 2003 Bren naming gift, when the school was simply the "School of Information and Computer Science").

**IoT** - Internet of Things. Theme of multiple BBComp years, especially 2016 and 2022 (Consense).

**LLM** - Large Language Model. Two Cube (2024 Butterworth 2nd) uses LLMs for autonomous bioinformatics analysis.

**NVC** - New Venture Competition. UCI's Stella Zhang NVC.

**RFID** - Radio Frequency Identification. Used in 2012's Rims inventory-management finalist team.

**TonoLase** - the at-home non-invasive intraocular-pressure monitoring device built by 2021's forMED Technologies (Beall 1st).

**USDA** - U.S. Department of Agriculture. Awarded a $250K Farmers Market Promotion Program grant to Leprendo (2022 Butterworth 1st) in December 2024.
