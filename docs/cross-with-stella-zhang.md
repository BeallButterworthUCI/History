# BBComp <-> Stella Zhang NVC Cross-Reference

[← Back to main archive](../README.md)

The two UCI student innovation competitions - the Beall Butterworth Competition (BBComp) and the Stella Zhang New Venture Competition (NVC) - share calendars, kickoff events, and a substantial overlap in competing teams and founders. This document systematically catalogs that overlap.

The companion archive at [StellaZhangUCI/History](https://github.com/StellaZhangUCI/History) covers the NVC in the same format used here for BBComp. The data below was computed by walking both `data/bbcomp.json` (this repo) and `data/nvc.json` (the sister repo).

## Founders Who Appeared in Both Archives Under Different Team Names

These founders competed at one event with one team and at the other event (or the same event in a different year) with a different team. They reveal the longest cross-competition career arcs.

### Zarina Bahadur - ZOOT (BBComp 2018) → Baby Box / 123 Baby Box (NVC 2020)

- **2018 BBComp Butterworth 1st:** ZOOT (AR app for finding lost belongings) - $10,000.
- **2020 NVC Grand Prize Winner:** Baby Box / 123 Baby Box (AI-powered subscription for age-tailored baby products, 0-3 years) - **$25,000 total**, including Consumer Products 1st.
- **Distinction:** Bahadur was the **first Master of Innovation and Entrepreneurship (MIE) student** to win the NVC (15th annual edition).
- **Subsequent:** the company also won a $50,000 TV pitch contest. Bahadur is now Founder & CEO of 123 Baby Box.
- **Source:** [merage.uci.edu, June 2020](https://merage.uci.edu/news/2020/06/baby-box-wins-25,000-grand-prize-at-2020-new-venture-competition.html); [innovation.uci.edu - 123 Baby Box TV pitch win](https://innovation.uci.edu/news/123-baby-box-wins-t-v-show-pitch-contest-and-50k/).

### Karan Kanwar & Martin Gomez - Pippy (BBComp 2017) → Wing (NVC 2018)

- **2017 BBComp Butterworth 3rd:** Pippy (AI-powered "on-demand anything" task-fulfillment marketplace) - $2,500.
- **2018 NVC Consumer Services 1st:** Wing (tech for corporate sustainable competitiveness) - $10,000. Team grew to 5: Karan Kanwar, Martin Gomez, Tod Srisengfa, Monish Ramadoss, Jackson Wickman.

### Kanu Chandra - Leprendo (BBComp 2022 → NVC 2023) → Oslo (BBComp 2025)

- **2022 BBComp Butterworth 1st:** Leprendo (online farmer's marketplace).
- **2023 NVC Grand Finale Runner-Up + Consumer Services 1st:** Leprendo (continued).
- **2025 BBComp International Collaboration 1st:** Oslo / Birdhouse by Oslo (contact-free thermal vital-sign monitoring for senior care). Different product, different team composition (with PUCRS partners).

### Dylan Riffle - Leprendo (BBComp 2022 → NVC 2023) → Two Cube (BBComp 2024 → NVC 2024)

- **2022 BBComp Butterworth 1st:** Leprendo.
- **2023 NVC Grand Finale Runner-Up + Consumer Services 1st:** Leprendo.
- **2024 BBComp Butterworth 2nd:** Two Cube (autonomous LLM-driven bioinformatics for single-cell RNA).
- **2024 NVC Life Sciences 1st:** Two Cube. Riffle is the only documented BBComp founder with two consecutive winning ventures across both archives.

### Catlin Tran - Nutripair (BBComp 2021 → NVC 2022)

- **2021 BBComp Butterworth 2nd:** Nutripair.
- **2022 NVC Business Products 1st ($10,000):** Nutripair (food-pairing technology for restaurants). Tran '23 specifically named in the 2022 NVC press release as the winner.
