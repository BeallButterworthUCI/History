# Where Are They Now?

[← Back to main archive](../README.md)

Most BBComp teams are senior-design or capstone projects whose lifespan ends with graduation. A smaller number incorporate, raise follow-on capital, or pivot into adjacent programs. This document tracks what we can verify from public sources.

**Note:** this is not a full alumni directory. Entries here are limited to teams with a publicly verifiable post-competition signal - a live company website, a follow-on competition win, a journalistic article, an SEC/Crunchbase entry, or a public LinkedIn role at a downstream company. We deliberately exclude private rumors, unconfirmed acquisitions, and anything sourced only to a personal blog. To add an entry or correct one, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

- **Status:** **active Y Combinator-backed company (Summer 2020 batch / S20)**, **14 employees (9 UCI alumni including 5 PhDs)**, FDA clearance anticipated within 2026; product expansion roadmap includes **home sleep monitoring**.
- **Site:** [Y Combinator profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/vena-vitals); [University Lab Partners profile](https://sites.uci.edu/mengprojects/vena-vitals/).

  - **Ray Liu** (CEO) - experienced medtech executive; met Khine 20+ years ago as UC Berkeley grad-school lab mates. **Education: Harvard Business School MBA + UC Berkeley MS Electrical Engineering + UIUC BS Electrical Engineering.** **20+ years experience in digital health and medical devices.** Previously head of product for two healthtech startups: **Ediom** (hospital decision-support tools, acquired by **Vizient**) and **Socialwellth** (consumer health engagement platform that reached **15.2M users**, acquired by **Cigna**); deep medical-device experience at **Siemens and GE Healthcare** (patient monitoring, healthcare diagnostics, ultrasound). Also developed large-scale digital challenge platforms used by Disney
  - **Joshua "Josh" Kim** (CTO and Co-Founder) - the same Joshua Kim from the 2019 BBComp Qardiac team; **UCI Materials Science and Engineering PhD with Prof. Michelle Khine as faculty advisor**; developed the underlying technology for his PhD; lead author on seminal papers
  - **Prof. Michelle Khine** - the recurring BBComp faculty mentor (also of 2014's Spero Diagnostics and A Hundred Tiny Hands); her lab developed the underlying soft, stretchable electronics technology
- **Team:** 14 employees total; 9 UCI alumni including 5 PhDs.
- **Product:** the Qardiac concept made commercial - a small skin-like sensor that adheres like a Band-Aid and captures blood pressure continuously, beat by beat, without needles, cuffs, or bulky equipment.
- **Funding:** total raised is not consistently reported across sources (CB Insights as of mid-2025 surfaces a single round at ~$130K; PitchBook profile exists; older 2023 industry summaries cited $1.43M and a May 2025 industry report references a separate $10M round; reconciling these would require Crunchbase / PitchBook direct subscription access). With the new lab operational, the company is now focused on completing clinical trials and obtaining FDA regulatory approval, with **market launch targeted for late 2027**. Future product roadmap: expand the device to monitor **heart rate variability and oxygen saturation** in addition to blood pressure.

### Syntr Health Technologies - 2017 Beall 3rd place

- **Status:** **active commercial company with $2.5M+ in disclosed funding**.
- **Founded:** 2016. The Syntrfuge concept was conceived during a senior-year class in 2015.
- **Site:** [syntrhealth.com](https://syntrhealth.com/about/our-team/); [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ahmedzobi/); [Preqin profile](https://www.preqin.com/data/profile/asset/syntr-health-technologies-inc-/511496).
- **Founders:** **Ahmed Zobi (CEO)**, Hugo Salas, Justin Stovner (the original 2017 BBComp Beall 3rd team).
- **Product:** **Syntrfuge** - an automated fat-processing system. A patient's own fat cells are extracted, activated, and re-injected near a damaged area in under an hour (process takes under 10 minutes for the fat processing itself), spurring growth of healthy new tissue. Single-use, disposable medical device. Targeted indications: facial fat loss, knee osteoarthritis, and diabetic foot ulcers. **FDA-cleared** per the Syntr Health website badge; Syntr's own marketing language is **"The World's First and Only Automated Microfat Processing System"** (per [syntrhealth.com](https://syntrhealth.com/)).
- **Funding:**
  - **$300,000 Phase I SBIR grant** from the U.S. Small Business Administration via the National Institutes of Health (October 2017).
  - **$2.2M seed round in August 2021**.
  - **$10M raise announced March 2023** (per Orange County Business Journal); funds support sales team hiring and marketing in 2023, expansion anticipated in 2024.
- **Headquarters:** Irvine, at University Lab Partners (UCI Research Park).
- **Press:** [innovation.uci.edu, October 2017 - $300K NIH grant](https://innovation.uci.edu/2017/10/uci-startup-receives-300000-grant-from-national-institutes-of-health/), [news.uci.edu "Wound warriors"](https://news.uci.edu/2018/01/22/wound-warriors/), [innovation.uci.edu BioAccel Solutions Challenge profile](https://innovation.uci.edu/2016/11/20161129making-waves-syntr-health-technologies/), [engineering.uci.edu "$10M raise" March 2023](https://engineering.uci.edu/syntr-health-raising-10m-fat-processing-device).
