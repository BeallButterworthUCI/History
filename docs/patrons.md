# The Patrons: Beall and Butterworth Families

[← Back to main archive](../README.md)

The UCI Beall Butterworth Competition exists because two families chose to fund student innovation at UCI for the long haul. This document collects the verified biographical and philanthropic record for both - see the source links at the end of each section for primary citations.

## Paul and Jo Butterworth (Butterworth track, ICS)

### Paul Butterworth - biography

- **Education:** B.S. Computer Science, UCI 1974; M.S. Computer Science, UCI 1981. Originally enrolled as an Engineering undergraduate in 1969 and switched to Computer Science in his third year.
- **Reflection on his own UCI experience:** *"I was considering not going to the university unless I received financial aid, because I didn't have any money... This is what inspired us to pledge support for students - so they can follow their dreams despite their financial situations."* (per [news.uci.edu, February 2023](https://news.uci.edu/2023/02/13/uc-irvine-alumni-paul-and-jo-butterworth-pledge-35-5-million-to-donald-bren-school/))

### Career timeline

| Stage | Company | Role |
|-------|---------|------|
| Early career | Ingres | Chief Systems Architect |
| 1991-1999 | Forte Software | Founder, Chief Architect, SVP - acquired by Sun Microsystems in October 1999 (~$540M in stock) |
| 1999-~2003 | Sun Microsystems | Distinguished Engineer, Chief Technologist (post-Forte acquisition) |

### UCI service & honors

- ICS Dean's Leadership Council - since 2005
- UCI Foundation Board of Trustees - since 2011
- UCI Audit Committee - since 2017
- UCI Lauds & Laurels Distinguished Alumnus - 2005
- Donald Bren School of ICS Hall of Fame inductee - 2015
- UCI Alumni Association Extraordinarius Award (UCI's highest alumni honor) - 2023, jointly with Jo Butterworth

### Jo Butterworth - biography

- **Education:** B.A. School of Social Sciences, UCI 1975.
- **Joint role:** has co-led the Butterworth philanthropic strategy at UCI alongside Paul.

### The 2023 pledge

On **13 February 2023**, Paul and Jo Butterworth pledged an estate gift valued at approximately **$35.5 million** to the Donald Bren School of ICS. At the time of announcement, this was the **largest gift to UCI from alumni in the university's history**.

The funds support:

- Scholarships and fellowships for students.
- New faculty hiring.
- Research initiatives aligned with the school's strategic priorities.
- Faculty chairs.

Sponsorship of the Butterworth Product Development Competition (which the Butterworths have funded since approximately 2003-2004) continues alongside the larger endowment.

## Donald R. Beall and the Beall Family Foundation (Beall track, Engineering)

### Donald R. Beall - biography

- **Education:** B.S. Metallurgical Engineering, San Jose State University (1960); M.B.A. with honors, University of Pittsburgh (1961).
- **Career:** 30-year career at Rockwell International, retired 1998. Served as either Chairman and CEO or President and COO for 20 of those years.
- **Current roles:** Chairman of the Beall Family Foundation; principal in Dartbrook Partners LLC; board positions including Rockwell Collins and CT Realty.

### Beall Family philanthropy at UCI

Total approximately **$30 million** as of 2019, including:

| Year | Gift | Result |
|------|------|--------|
| 2000 | Founding gift from Rockwell International (honoring Beall's retirement) | Beall Center for Art + Technology opens at the Claire Trevor School of the Arts |
| 2007 | $5,000,000 | Beall Center for Innovation and Entrepreneurship founded at the Paul Merage School of Business |
| 2014 | Sponsorship | Beall Student Design Competition launches at the Henry Samueli School of Engineering |
| 2018 | Operational support | Son Kenneth Beall appointed chairman of UCI's entrepreneurial hub; helps launch a master's in innovation & entrepreneurship at Merage (the only such program in the UC system at the time) |
| 2019 | Multimillion-dollar gift | UCI Applied Innovation renamed UCI Beall Applied Innovation |

### Beall philanthropy outside UCI

- Don Beall Dean of Engineering chair at San Jose State University.
- Donald R. Beall Chair in Strategic Management at the University of Pittsburgh.
