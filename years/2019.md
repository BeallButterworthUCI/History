# 2019 UCI Beall Butterworth Competition

[← Back to main archive](../README.md)

**Theme:** Mobile Health Data and Wearable Cardiology.

## Awards

| Track | Place | Team | Prize | Project Details | Team Members |
|-------|-------|------|-------|-----------------|--------------|
| Butterworth | 1st | CheckUP | $10,000 | A health-data collection mobile app that lets consumers order blood and immunology tests, with HIPAA-compliant storage and IBM Watson Health integration. | Zachary Little, Kenneth Rhee, Dan Appel, Francisco Barcena |
| Butterworth | 2nd | Giv | $6,500 | A mobile-first donation platform that lowers friction for charitable giving. | Lucas Erb, Haven King, Ryan Luu, Nisarg Shah |
| Butterworth | 3rd | Pyri | $3,500 | A foreign-currency exchange service operated through travel lodging partners. | Salahaldeen Ali, Niijan Al-Amin |
| Beall | 1st | Qardiac | $10,000 | A soft pressure-sensor "bandage" blood-pressure monitor with cloud transmission for continuous cardiac monitoring. | Joshua Kim, Lancy Lin, Sophia Chou, Uuanke Zhang |
| Beall | 2nd | CenSyn | $6,500 | A portable EEG device paired with a smartphone app for neurological monitoring outside of clinical settings. | Ayushi Hitesh Patel, Trevor Gray Silence, Anirudh Bhushan, Hengji Chen, Quinn Korematsu, Raj Parekh |
| Beall | 3rd | HemaDynamics | $3,500 | A handheld laser-speckle blood-flow imaging device for diabetic-foot-ulcer monitoring. | Adrian Bahani, Emil Lundqvist, Steven Chang, Stephanie Pascua, Natasha Palamuttam, Dian Song |

## Notes

- The official BBComp page renders the team name as **CheckUP** (capital U-P). Some secondary sources record it as "Team CheckUp" — they refer to the same team.
- Adrian Bahani (HemaDynamics 2019) was previously on the 2017 Beall 3rd-place team Syntr Health Technologies, making him one of the rare individuals to place in two separate years.
- Per-placement prize amounts are recorded as $10K / $6.5K / $3.5K consistent with the post-2018 BBComp prize structure on the rules page; the 2019 results page does not enumerate dollar figures explicitly.
- Source: [bbcomp.tech.uci.edu/past/past-2019/](https://bbcomp.tech.uci.edu/past/past-2019/).
