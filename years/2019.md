## Notes

- **Vena Vitals is now an active Y Combinator-backed company.** The 2019 Beall 1st-place team's Qardiac technology - a soft, skin-like, Band-Aid-style continuous blood-pressure sensor - was commercialized as Vena Vitals, founded in 2019 by **Joshua Kim** (CTO and Co-Founder, the BBComp Qardiac team lead), **Michelle Khine** (Co-Founder, recurring BBComp faculty mentor whose lab developed the underlying soft electronics), and **Ray Liu** (CEO; UC Berkeley grad-school lab mate of Khine 20+ years prior). Investors include Morado Ventures, Founders Inc., Y Combinator, MedTech Innovator, EvoNexus, plus a Beall Applied Innovation PoP grant. **600+ patients tested in operating rooms; FDA clearance anticipated 2026.** Won the 2023 University Lab Partners "Pitch. Launch. Grow." Orange County competition (MedTech Track). 14 employees today. Source: [innovation.uci.edu](https://innovation.uci.edu/news/vena-vitals/), [Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/vena-vitals), [Morado Ventures](https://blog.moradoventures.com/read/why-we-invested-vena-vitals/).
- **CenSyn Inc. is now an active company.** The 2019 Beall 2nd-place team (Ayushi Patel, Andy Bhushan, Trevor Silence) immediately founded CenSyn after the competition. Product: the **CenSyn PenEEG**, a pen-sized portable EEG. Funding: **$105K Seed from Berkeley SkyDeck** (September 28, 2021), plus the Arizona Commerce Authority, 1517 Fund, HonorHealth, Monsoon Venture Fund, and Skydeck Capital. Executive Chairman: **Peter Crosby** (40 years medical-device industry). Team grew to 11 members; pursuing FDA 510(k) clearance. Sources: [innovation.uci.edu, Jan 2021](https://innovation.uci.edu/2021/01/censyn-brings-brain-health-to-front-of-mind/), [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/censyn).
- The official BBComp page renders the team name as **CheckUP** (capital U-P) and the 3rd-place Beall team as **HemaDycamics** (one source) versus **HemaDynamics** (other sources); we use HemaDynamics as the canonical project name since it is the form the team itself adopted.
- Adrian Bahani (HemaDynamics 2019) was previously on the 2017 Beall 3rd-place team Syntr Health Technologies, making him one of the rare individuals to place in two separate years.
- **AllerChain** (2019 combined-track finalist) shares its core members with the 2021 Beall 1st-place winner **forMED Technologies** - Lily McGrale, Aryan Agarwal, Ruben Shakya, Raphael Francis Aguas, and Kamalesh Ananthakrishnan all appear on both rosters. AllerChain is effectively the precursor team to forMED.
- **Lucas Erb, Nisarg Shah, Haven King** (Giv 2019 Butterworth 2nd) were previously the 2018 Butterworth UnMail finalist team - same trio, new product.
- **Kausthub Raj Jadhav** (Float Robotics 2019 combined finalist) was on the 2017 Butterworth 3rd-place Pippy team and the 2016 Butterworth FASHENDS finalist team.
- **Gautam Banuru & Swapnil Khodaskar** (ChangeNOW finalist 2019) were also on the 2018 RelaxTech and CodeRight finalist teams.
- Per-placement prize amounts are recorded as $10K / $6.5K / $3.5K consistent with the post-2018 BBComp prize structure on the rules page; the 2019 results page does not enumerate dollar figures explicitly.
- Source: [bbcomp.tech.uci.edu/past/past-2019/](https://bbcomp.tech.uci.edu/past/past-2019/).
