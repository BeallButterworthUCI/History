# 2018 UCI Beall Butterworth Competition

[← Back to main archive](../README.md)

**Theme:** Augmented Reality and Engineering for Healthcare Workflows.
**Notable:** first year at the higher prize tier ($10,000 / $6,500 / $3,500), up from the original $7,500 / $5,000 / $2,500 used 2014-2017.

## Awards

| Track | Place | Team | Prize | Project Details | Team Members |
|-------|-------|------|-------|-----------------|--------------|
| Butterworth | 1st | ZOOT | $10,000 | An augmented-reality mobile app for finding misplaced household belongings via spatial recall. Once the Zoot app locates a lost item, the AR feature gives the user an approximate range (specific room, multi-level building, etc.). Started as Bahadur's vision of helping drivers find parked cars. | Zarina Bahadur, Ting-Wei Lin (Informatics), Roger Dalke (last-minute coding addition) |
| Butterworth | 2nd | CrewView | $6,500 | A cross-platform AR / VR collaboration tool for creative teams to review work in immersive environments. | Ke Jing, Christopher Ayuso, Max Collins, Melisse Yutuc, Aparajita Marathe, Fernando Penaloza, Tyler Scrivner, Hannah Tran |
| Butterworth | 3rd | Breakdown | $3,500 | A political-information-aggregator web app that summarizes legislation and elections in plain language. | Ryan Oillataguerre, Brodric Cormier |
| Beall | 1st | FWMAV | $10,000 | A flapping-wing micro-aerial vehicle that replaces propellers with mechanical wings, enabling non-invasive close-quarters drone flight. | Bao Pham, Nathan Cabezut, Patrick Zhu |
| Beall | 2nd | HUMBLE Technologies | $6,500 | A re-engineered syringe that eliminates priming-related dosage errors. **Now operating as a Delaware C corporation** (officially formed February 9, 2018), 100% owned by UCI and San Diego State University undergraduates. CEO and principal inventor: Bien Gutierrez. Won TCU Values & Ventures 2nd ($750) in 2018. Site: [humbletech.uci.design](http://www.humbletech.uci.design/). | Bien Gutierrez, Wendy Nguyen, Jonathan Enriquez, Xiantong Yang, Chad Bishop, Krissa Tassin |
| Beall | 3rd | Mechanodontics | $3,500 | Customized braces engineered to halve orthodontic treatment time (behind-the-teeth braces reducing treatment to ~1 year). Same team also won the **2018 Stella Zhang NVC Life Sciences 1st** with extended roster (some surnames appear differently in the two competition rosters). | Zahra Mardy, Mahdi Abbaspour, James Wratten, Mehdi Roein-Peikar |

## Notes

- 2018 marked the prize-amount step-up from the original $7.5K / $5K / $2.5K structure used in the inaugural Beall years (2014-2017) to the $10K / $6.5K / $3.5K structure that has held through 2025.
- **Zarina Bahadur (ZOOT 2018 Butterworth 1st)** later enrolled in the inaugural class of the Master of Innovation and Entrepreneurship (MIE) program at the Paul Merage School and **founded 123 Baby Box**. In 2020 she won the **Stella Zhang NVC Grand Prize ($25,000 total - Grand Prize plus Consumer Products 1st), the first-ever MIE student to win the NVC**. The company also won a $50,000 TV pitch contest. **In April 2023 the company raised an oversubscribed $1.2M pre-seed round** (total raised $1.3M+) led by XRC Labs with participation from Sunstone Fund, Salt Lake City Angels, VC California Crescent, plus angels Mark Friedman (ex-Steve Madden) and Demos Parneros (ex-Barnes & Noble CEO). Targeting Series A metrics of $100K MRR / $1M ARR. Press: [TechCrunch March 2023](https://techcrunch.com/2023/03/13/123-baby-box-subscription-commerce/), [OC Business Journal](https://www.ocbj.com/oc-homepage/123-baby-box-raises-1-2m-pre-seed/), [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/123-baby-box). Source for original 2020 NVC win: [merage.uci.edu](https://merage.uci.edu/news/2020/06/baby-box-wins-25,000-grand-prize-at-2020-new-venture-competition.html).
- **Mechanodontics (Beall 3rd)** also won the **2018 Stella Zhang NVC Life Sciences 1st place** with an extended roster.
- **Lucas Erb, Nisarg Shah, Haven King** entered as the UnMail finalist team in 2018 and returned in 2019 as the Butterworth 2nd-place team **Giv** - one of the clearest year-over-year trajectories in the archive.
- **Cass Tao** placed in the 2017 STACK finalist team and returned in 2018 with the Sprankles finalist team.
- The 2018 cycle was the year **Mechanodontics** added members **James Wratten** and **Mehdi Roein-Peikar** to the dual-competition team registration; the awards roster credits Zahra Mardy and Mahdi Abbaspour as the final placement team.
- Source: [bbcomp.tech.uci.edu/past/2018-participants-and-results/](https://bbcomp.tech.uci.edu/past/2018-participants-and-results/), [ics.uci.edu, June 2018](https://ics.uci.edu/archive-news/?id=1380).
