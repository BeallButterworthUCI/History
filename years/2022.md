# 2022 UCI Beall Butterworth Competition

[← Back to main archive](../README.md)

**Theme:** Marketplaces, Assistive Hardware, and the Return to In-Person.
**Total prize purse:** more than $40,000 (per ics.uci.edu).
**Notable:** first fully in-person ceremony post-pandemic, after the 2020 cancellation and the 2021 virtual edition.

## Awards

| Track | Place | Team | Prize | Project Details | Team Members |
|-------|-------|------|-------|-----------------|--------------|
| Butterworth | 1st | Leprendo | $10,000 | An online farmer's marketplace that connects specialty farmers directly to consumers, bypassing inefficient distribution networks. Continues to operate post-graduation; recipient of a $250,000 USDA Farmers Market Promotion Program grant. | Dylan Riffle, Kanu Chandra, Dan Ta, Nina Nguyen |
| Butterworth | 2nd | Foodpool | $6,500 | A "dining hall on wheels" food-delivery app for college students, helping the UCI community fuel their bodies without breaking the bank. Famously pivoted late and used a low-fidelity prototype "Hail Mary" pitch. | Sanghyun Byun, Arthur Lafrance, Patrick Wang, Kevin Xu |
| Butterworth | 3rd | Consense | $3,500 | An Internet-of-Things system that leverages crowd-sourced sensor data to make cities safer for cyclists. | Richard DeAmicis, Lily He, Ado Ibori, Melissa Yuenn |
| Beall | 1st | Idene Medical | $10,000 | A patent-pending wheelchair assist handle / floor-to-wheelchair transfer aid that extends the range of mobility for wheelchair users. | Raniel Calilung, Sang Hee Kim, Evelyn Novoa, Sam Rostami |
| Beall | 2nd | VANTAMASK | $6,500 | An electric reusable consumer-grade mask. | Nnaoma Agwu, Daryl Chau |
| Beall | 3rd | Thrust Aeronautics | $3,500 | A toroidal aerospike for use in liquid-fueled rockets. | Isaac Larroque, Michael Ross |

## Notes

- **Leprendo** continues to operate at [leprendo.com](https://leprendo.com/about). Reported $400,000 first-year sales and received a $250,000 USDA Farmers Market Promotion Program grant. Also won the UCI Stella Zhang New Venture Competition that year. Source: [antrepreneur.uci.edu, October 2022](https://antrepreneur.uci.edu/2022/10/18/interview-with-antrepreneurs-dan-ta-and-dylan-riffle-of-leprendo/).
- **Idene Medical** has a UC eScholarship publication detailing the wheelchair assist handle: [escholarship.org/uc/item/7b9348x2](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/7b9348x2).
- Michael Ross (Thrust Aeronautics 2022) later co-founded **Sentinel**, the 2024 Butterworth 1st-place team.
- Dylan Riffle and Kanu Chandra (both Leprendo) continued to compete in later cycles — Riffle won 2nd place in 2024 with Two Cube, Chandra won the 2025 International Collaboration Prize with Oslo.
- Source: [bbcomp.tech.uci.edu/past/past-2022/](https://bbcomp.tech.uci.edu/past/past-2022/), [engineering.uci.edu, June 2022](https://engineering.uci.edu/news/2022/6/beall-and-butterworth-competitions-winners-announced), [ics.uci.edu, June 2022](https://ics.uci.edu/2022/06/23/2022-butterworth-product-development-competition-back-in-full-swing/).
