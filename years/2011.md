# 2011 UCI Butterworth Product Development Competition

[← Back to main archive](../README.md)

**Track:** Butterworth-only.
**Edition:** 8th annual Butterworth Product Development Competition.
**Status:** **No public record found** of placements, team names, or members.

A web search suggests "the top three teams in the 2011 Butterworth Product Development Competition were recognized in a June awards ceremony," confirming the event happened, but the actual results page is not currently accessible - the legacy ICS news archive returns broken links for the relevant article ID range.

## What we know

- The competition was held and a top three was recognized in June 2011.
- Paul Butterworth was the named sponsor.
- The likely prize tier was the same $5,000 / $3,500 / $1,500 structure used in the verified 2012 and 2013 cycles, but this is not directly confirmed for 2011.
