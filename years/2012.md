# 2012 UCI Butterworth Product Development Competition

[← Back to main archive](../README.md)

**Track:** Butterworth-only. The Beall Student Design Competition did not yet exist — it launched in 2014.
**Edition:** 9th annual Butterworth Product Development Competition.
**Final round:** May 17, 2012, in Bren Hall.
**Format:** began in January with 19 teams and over 60 students; 10 finalist teams competed in the final round.
**Total prize purse:** $10,000.
**Prize tier:** $5,000 (1st) / $3,500 (2nd) / $1,500 (3rd) — the original Butterworth-era structure used 2012 and 2013 before the 2014 joint edition raised it.

## Notes

- Judge **Kevin Kinsey** (then CEO and co-founder of Netreo) on the 2012 cohort: *"This was, by far, the most impressive group of competitors we've seen over the last nine years."* The "nine years" framing locks the inaugural Butterworth Product Development Competition to ~2003–2004.
- The competition was named after Bren School alumnus **Paul Butterworth** (B.S. '74, M.S. '81), who was at the time CTO of AccessG2 Inc.
- Co-founder **Danielle Yu** of Portfelo has been featured in UCI Informatics undergraduate alumni spotlights.
- Mentor **Tony Crisp** later became a permanent BBComp judge.
- Mentor **Channa Samynathan** continued mentoring teams through at least 2014 (Sparky / Blueberry).
