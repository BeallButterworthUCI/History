# 2024 UCI Beall Butterworth Competition

[← Back to main archive](../README.md)

**Theme:** AI Safety, Additive Manufacturing, and B2B Marketplaces.
**Scale:** 56 competition concepts narrowed to 33 Demo Day projects - director David Ochi noted the participant count "eclipsed any previous year by over 25%."

## Awards

| Track | Place | Team | Prize | Project Details | Team Members |
|-------|-------|------|-------|-----------------|--------------|
| Butterworth | 1st | Sentinel | $10,000 | The first driver-conscious AI safety system to detect fatigue and distraction. Targets vehicle fleets with high operator liability (e.g., trucking) to help fleet managers and drivers improve road safety. **Now commercialized as Spotter AI's Sentinel platform** at [sentinel.spotter.ai](https://sentinel.spotter.ai/) - see notes. | Cody He, Kevin Wu, Kyle Deck, Michael Ross, Natán Ampudia, Spike O'Carroll |
| Butterworth | 2nd | Two Cube | $6,500 | An autonomous bioinformatics analysis platform that runs single-cell RNA pipelines without manual setup. Uses Large Language Models for scRNA code generation and analysis ("the autonomous bioinformatician"). Later won 1st in the Stella Zhang NVC Life Science track ($10,000). | Dylan Riffle, Shushrruth Sai Srinivasan |
| Butterworth | 3rd | Biru | $3,500 | A maid- and household-help-matching platform tailored to the Indonesian market. | Jessica Hardisurjo, Kevin Salim, Nicholas Chairnando, Pierre Theodorus, William Hartono |

## Notes

- Sentinel went on to win the 2024 UCI Stella Zhang New Venture Competition Grand Finale ($20K + $10K track) and placed 2nd in the C&C Innovative Solutions SoCal Super Pitch - one of the strongest cross-competition records in BBComp history. Source: [antrepreneur.uci.edu, September 2024](https://antrepreneur.uci.edu/2024/09/12/the-power-of-persistence-how-uc-irvine-entrepreneurs-built-success-through-multiple-competitions/).
- **Sentinel is now a commercial product as the Sentinel platform from Spotter AI** ([sentinel.spotter.ai](https://sentinel.spotter.ai/)) - an all-in-one fleet safety platform with real-time driver monitoring, DOT compliance alerts, and AI-powered hiring/screening tools. Listed President: Gabriel Stonys. Sources: [Grit Daily, 2025](https://gritdaily.com/spotter-ais-sentinel-simplifies-driver-hiring/), [Industry Today](https://industrytoday.com/spotter-ai-launches-sentinel-to-cut-hiring-costs/), [TheTrucker.com](https://www.thetrucker.com/trucking-news/business/team-behind-big-rig-safety-ai-wins-stella-zhang-competition-grand-prize).
- The Sentinel pitch cited "over 500,000 annual semi-truck accidents causing nearly 6,000 deaths and $91,000 per-accident costs to companies."
- Michael Ross also competed on the 2022 Beall 3rd-place team Thrust Aeronautics, making him a two-time placement winner across both tracks.
- Dylan Riffle was previously on the 2022 Butterworth 1st-place Leprendo team.
- NeoMold maintains a project page at [neomold.uci.design](http://neomold.uci.design/) and has an associated UC eScholarship publication ([escholarship.org/uc/item/5fs7b1cr](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/5fs7b1cr)). The product targets ~1 in 6,000 newborns with congenital ear deformities. The team also reached the **2024 UCI Stella Zhang NVC top 10 finalists** (out of 125 teams from 400+ applicants) and **placed top 2 in the Life Sciences track**. Christine Ly graduated UCI BME in June 2024 and joined the **USC BioMEMS lab as a PhD student** in August 2024.
- **Two Cube** (2024 Butterworth 2nd) followed up by winning **1st in the Stella Zhang NVC Life Science track ($10,000)** - making 2024 a year where both the 1st and 2nd place Butterworth winners (Sentinel and Two Cube) plus the 1st place Beall winner (NeoMold) all went on to place in the Stella Zhang NVC. Co-founder **Shushrruth Sai Srinivasan** is a PhD student in CS at UCI with peer-reviewed publications in single-cell epigenetic data analysis; presented at the 2025 Health Care Forecast Conference.
- 2024 was the second year of the UCI / PUCRS Brazil partnership.
- Sources: [bbcomp.tech.uci.edu/past/past-2024/](https://bbcomp.tech.uci.edu/past/past-2024/), [engineering.uci.edu, June 2024](https://engineering.uci.edu/news/2024/6/students-their-game-2024-beall-butterworth-competition).
