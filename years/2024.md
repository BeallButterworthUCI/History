# 2024 UCI Beall Butterworth Competition

[← Back to main archive](../README.md)

**Theme:** AI Safety, Additive Manufacturing, and B2B Marketplaces.
**Scale:** 56 competition concepts narrowed to 33 Demo Day projects - director David Ochi noted the participant count "eclipsed any previous year by over 25%."

## Notes

- Sentinel went on to win the 2024 UCI Stella Zhang New Venture Competition Grand Finale ($20K + $10K track) and placed 2nd in the C&C Innovative Solutions SoCal Super Pitch - one of the strongest cross-competition records in BBComp history. Source: [antrepreneur.uci.edu, September 2024](https://antrepreneur.uci.edu/2024/09/12/the-power-of-persistence-how-uc-irvine-entrepreneurs-built-success-through-multiple-competitions/).
- **Sentinel is now a commercial product as the Sentinel platform from Spotter AI** ([sentinel.spotter.ai](https://sentinel.spotter.ai/)) - an all-in-one fleet safety platform with real-time driver monitoring, DOT compliance alerts, and AI-powered hiring/screening tools. Listed President: Gabriel Stonys. **Public commercial launch on February 17, 2026** as the AI-powered hiring/screening/compliance platform; **claims to cut driver hiring and screening costs by up to 75%**. **March 31, 2026 platform update** added a digital driver application that streamlines recruiting, compliance, and onboarding in one workflow with branded applications and multi-channel form distribution. Sources: [Grit Daily, 2025](https://gritdaily.com/spotter-ais-sentinel-simplifies-driver-hiring/), [Industry Today](https://industrytoday.com/spotter-ai-launches-sentinel-to-cut-hiring-costs/), [TheTrucker.com](https://www.thetrucker.com/trucking-news/business/team-behind-big-rig-safety-ai-wins-stella-zhang-competition-grand-prize), [National Law Review, March 2026](https://natlawreview.com/press-releases/spotter-ai-expands-sentinel-platform-ai-powered-hiring-verification-and).
- The Sentinel pitch cited "over 500,000 annual semi-truck accidents causing nearly 6,000 deaths and $91,000 per-accident costs to companies."
- Michael Ross also competed on the 2022 Beall 3rd-place team Thrust Aeronautics, making him a two-time placement winner across both tracks.
- Dylan Riffle was previously on the 2022 Butterworth 1st-place Leprendo team.
- NeoMold maintains a project page at [neomold.uci.design](http://neomold.uci.design/) and has an associated UC eScholarship publication ([escholarship.org/uc/item/5fs7b1cr](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/5fs7b1cr)). The product targets ~1 in 6,000 newborns with congenital ear deformities. The team also reached the **2024 UCI Stella Zhang NVC top 10 finalists** (out of 125 teams from 400+ applicants) and **placed top 2 in the Life Sciences track**. Christine Ly graduated UCI BME in June 2024 and joined the **USC BioMEMS lab as a PhD student** in August 2024 with USC Annenberg and Viterbi Fellowships, focused on neural medical device development. **Christine Linh Nguyen** is now **Quality Engineer NPI at PROCEPT BioRobotics** (the commercial-stage surgical-robotics company, AQUABEAM Robotic System for BPH / enlarged-prostate treatment) and is concurrently pursuing a **Stanford MS in Bioengineering (2024-2025)**; prior internships/co-ops at Terumo Neuro, Medtronic, and UCI.
- **Two Cube** (2024 Butterworth 2nd) followed up by winning **1st in the Stella Zhang NVC Life Science track ($10,000)** - making 2024 a year where both the 1st and 2nd place Butterworth winners (Sentinel and Two Cube) plus the 1st place Beall winner (NeoMold) all went on to place in the Stella Zhang NVC. Co-founder **Shushrruth Sai Srinivasan** is a PhD student in **UCI's Mathematical, Computational, and Systems Biology (MCSB) Program**; research in Bioinformatics, Genomics, and Applied Machine Learning; peer-reviewed publications in Science Advances and Alzheimer's & Dementia (scENCORE chromatin conformation work); presented at the 2025 Health Care Forecast Conference.
- **Sentinel** was also selected for and graduated from the **NSF I-Corps Regional Program**, gaining technology-translation and market-validation training. Co-founder **Michael Ross** previously founded Thrust Aeronautics (2022 Beall 3rd) and conducted research on aerospike nozzles, aerospace alloys, and additive manufacturing techniques.
- **Spike O'Carroll** (Sentinel co-founder) is now a **Software Engineer at SpaceX building Starlink's payments infrastructure** (joined as Starship Software Engineering intern and converted to full-time). UCI CS, 3.95 GPA. Personal portfolio at [spike.codes](https://spike.codes/). Notable solo work outside Sentinel: created **Libreddit (now Redlib)** which reached **4M+ monthly active users**; built **Peacock Browser (30K downloads)**; and is founder of **Dispatch AI** (grants from **OpenAI, Intel, SkyDeck, and Mistral**). **$130K+ in total prize winnings** across hackathons and startup competitions. Alongside the 2024 Sentinel BBComp / Stella Zhang Grand Finale wins he also won 1st at the **UC Berkeley AI Hackathon** (DispatchAI), 1st at the **LAHacks Google Company Challenge** (AdaptEd), and 1st at the **UCI ANTrepreneur Center AI Innovation Challenge** Student Experience track (Notive, with Steven Cao, Sahas Kasuganti, Kevin Wu).

- **Kyle Deck** (Sentinel co-founder) was **Project Manager of UCI Rocket Project (Liquids)** for three years and led the team's second liquid-rocket launch **MOCH4 (May 2025)** pursuing the collegiate methalox world record.
