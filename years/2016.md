# 2016 UCI Beall Butterworth Competition

[← Back to main archive](../README.md)

**Theme:** The Internet of Things and Bluetooth Low Energy.
**Prize tier:** $7,500 / $5,000 / $2,500.

## Awards

| Track | Place | Team | Prize | Project Details | Team Members |
|-------|-------|------|-------|-----------------|--------------|

Team lead Forest Agostinelli is now **Assistant Professor of Computer Science and Engineering at the University of South Carolina**, focused on AI, Deep Learning, Reinforcement Learning, and Heuristic Search; 2,100+ scholarly citations. | Forest Agostinelli (lead), Ameya Raje, Chaiyathorn Vachirakornwattana, Prakul Agarwal |

| Beall | 1st | Tag Team | $7,500 | A small Bluetooth Low Energy device - "Tag" - used with a smartphone to help users secure belongings; also sends distress signals to emergency contacts and leverages a crowd-GPS network for lost items. (Predates Apple AirTag by ~5 years.) | Ali Shahbaz (lead), Khwaja Hassan Ahmad, Neeraj Kumar |
| Beall | 2nd | Red Lion Robotics | $5,000 | Accessible tools for physical rehabilitation and assistance. **Now an active company** with product **GRAM (Gait Rehab Adaptive Machine)** - a low-cost wheelchair attachment named after Yasemin's grandmother. Won 2017 Aging 2.0 Orange County regional business competition + advanced nationally. **Peter Wang** is now Research Staff Scientist at Oak Ridge National Laboratory. | Yasemin Sarigul-Klijn (Founder), Peter Wang (Lead Design Engineer), Brandon Tsuge |
| Beall | 3rd | CeleriBio | $2,500 | A rapid, quantitative diagnostic test for bloodstream infections and antibiotic resistance. **Same year, the team also won 2nd place + $5,000 at the 2016 UCI Business Plan Competition Life Sciences/Clean Tech track**, plus participated in the **UCI BioAccel Solutions Challenge** med-tech start-up program. CeleriBio itself did not commercialize, but team lead **Louai Labanieh** went on to a remarkable trajectory: **Stanford PhD (Crystal Mackall Lab + Parker Scholar)** in synthetic biology and protein engineering for CAR-T cells (drug-regulated switches and logic-gated protein circuits); **co-founder of CARGO Therapeutics in 2021** with Crystal Mackall, Nancy Goodman, and Robbie Majzner; his technologies were licensed to CARGO and supported a **Phase I clinical trial of the SNIP CAR platform in relapsed/refractory solid cancers**; **CARGO Therapeutics signed an agreement to be acquired by Concentra Biosciences on July 8, 2025 at $4.379/share + CVR (~$170.7M total per InsideArbitrage analyst calculation (the GlobeNewswire press release states only $4.379/share cash + CVR, not an aggregate)); the acquisition tender offer closed August 18, 2025; merger completed August 19, 2025 with the company delisted from Nasdaq**; he is now an **Assistant Professor at the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai**. | Sean Freeman, Nikki Koe, Louai Labanieh (lead), Binh Le, Sadaf Mirnia |

## Notes

- **Tag Team** built and launched a crowd-GPS Bluetooth tag system roughly five years before Apple's AirTag (2021), demonstrating the typical "ahead of the consumer market" pattern that recurs across Beall winners.
- **Red Lion Robotics** placed 2nd again in 2017 with a slightly different roster (Hwan Heo replacing Brandon Tsuge).
- **Krobotech** appeared as a finalist in 2016 (Ethan Kirkley, Cameron Hunt, Zepyoor Khechadoorian) and returned in 2017 to win Beall 1st with the addition of Kevin Wong - one of the clearest "near-miss this year, win next year" trajectories in the joint era.
- **Tu Nguyen** (Roco 2015 1st Butterworth, Tack 2016 finalist) and **Naomi Thomson** (HelioTeq 2015 finalist, Kneed Technologies 2016 finalist) are among the cross-year repeat appearers.
- **Memorlane** appeared as a 2016 Butterworth finalist (Evan Schein, Akshat Patel, Yocelyn Recinos) and returned in 2017 to win Butterworth 1st (with Michael Tran replacing Akshat Patel).
- **2016 is the cycle that produced the first documented BBComp-traceable M&A exit on record.** CeleriBio team lead Louai Labanieh went on to co-found CARGO Therapeutics in 2021; CARGO was acquired by Concentra Biosciences on August 19, 2025 at approximately $170.7M total ($4.379/share cash + CVR), delisting from Nasdaq.
- Source: [bbcomp.tech.uci.edu/past/2016-participants-and-results/](https://bbcomp.tech.uci.edu/past/2016-participants-and-results/), [engineering.uci.edu, June 2016](https://engineering.uci.edu/news/2016/6/beall-butterworth-student-design-competition-winners-announced), [globenewswire.com (CARGO acquisition agreement, July 8, 2025)](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/07/08/3111546/0/en/CARGO-Therapeutics-Enters-into-Agreement-to-Be-Acquired-by-Concentra-Biosciences-for-4-379-in-Cash-per-Share-Plus-a-Contingent-Value-Right.html).
