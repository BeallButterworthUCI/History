# 2015 UCI Beall Butterworth Competition

[← Back to main archive](../README.md)

**Theme:** Diagnostic Hardware and Mobile Software Mobility.
**Edition:** 12th annual Butterworth + 2nd annual Beall.
**Prize tier:** $7,500 (1st) / $5,000 (2nd) / $2,500 (3rd) per track.

> **Source disambiguation:** the official `bbcomp.tech.uci.edu/past/2015-participants-and-results/` and `…/2014-participants-and-results/` pages render duplicate content, both showing the 2015 winners. The actual 2014 winners (Spero Diagnostics, A Hundred Tiny Hands, HydroTrack, Sparky / Blueberry, BeatPool, Ant-mApp) are confirmed by the [Samueli July 2014 press release](https://engineering.uci.edu/news/2014/7/butterworth-and-beall-competitions-reward-best-both-hardware-and-software). The 2015 placements below are the canonical content of both bbcomp pages and are now treated as **verified** (the duplication points down - the bbcomp 2014 page is incorrect, not the 2015 one).

## Awards

| Track | Place | Team | Prize | Project Details | Team Members |
|-------|-------|------|-------|-----------------|--------------|
| Butterworth | 1st | Roco | $7,500 | An intelligent mobile application using machine learning to create an automated private photo-sharing system. Featured a unique face-scanning capability using one-shot learning combined with geofiltering for higher-accuracy facial recognition at registration. | Jenny Hua, Bing Hui Feng, Naren Sathiya, Tu Nguyen, Kasean Herrera |
| Butterworth | 2nd | NoteCycle (Quicknotes) | $5,000 | A crowdsourced classroom note platform - students who feel they take good notes can upload them; classmates access the notes through the platform. | Arvind Sontha, Ofri Harlev, Treston Mendonsa |
| Butterworth | 3rd | FanFeed! | $2,500 | A free mobile application (iOS and Android) enhancing the in-stadium fan experience by providing in-stadium food delivery and in-seat purchases, helping fans avoid long concession-stand lines. | Candace Wu, Ravi Patel, Brandon Troung |
| Beall | 1st | MuTech Scientific (DECAY-Z) | $7,500 | A device that promotes ease of access to HIV diagnostics by decreasing testing cost and complexity. | Ya Kevin Thao, Abraham Phung, Diego Sandoval, Eric Wilde, Zaw Mai, Cory Schoenborn, Michael Bulotano |
| Beall | 2nd | Sixth Sense (ECO) | $5,000 | The Energy Conservation Observer (ECO) - a context-aware device that accumulates a user's energy-consumption data to encourage conservation. | Shriti Raj, Neeraj Kumar, LouAnne Boyd, Ali Shahbaz |
| Beall | 3rd | OneSeed | $2,500 | A "smart" gardening system that removes the guesswork from gardening through sensor-driven plant care. | Stella Liu, Vouy Yeng, Sandy Pham, Aaron Rosario, Briana O'Hern, Erik Henriquez, Ryan Kuhs |

## Notes

- **Repeat appearances from 2014:** Neeraj Kumar (Sixth Sense 2nd Beall, 2015) won 1st Beall in 2016 with Tag Team. Ali Shahbaz (Sixth Sense 2nd Beall, 2015) led Tag Team to 1st Beall in 2016. Derek Omuro (ARIS finalist, 2015) was on the 2014 Butterworth 1st-place Sparky / Blueberry team. Michael Bryant (HelioTeq finalist, 2015) was on the 2014 Beall 3rd-place HydroTrack team.
- **Sparky / Blueberry alumni:** Sparky was 2014's Butterworth 1st-place team. Some members continued - Derek Omuro shows up on the 2015 ARIS finalist roster.
- **Roco's machine-learning angle** anticipates trends that became dominant in consumer apps several years later (one-shot facial recognition, geofiltering for content sharing).
- Source: [bbcomp.tech.uci.edu/past/2015-participants-and-results/](https://bbcomp.tech.uci.edu/past/2015-participants-and-results/).
