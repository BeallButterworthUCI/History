# 2013 UCI Butterworth Product Development Competition

[← Back to main archive](../README.md)

**Track:** Butterworth-only. The Beall Student Design Competition did not yet exist — it launched in 2014.
**Edition:** 10th annual Butterworth Product Development Competition.
**Prize tier:** $5,000 (1st) / $3,500 (2nd) / $1,500 (3rd) — the same structure used in 2012, the last year before the 2014 joint edition with the Beall track raised it to $7,500 / $5,000 / $2,500.

## Awards

| Track | Place | Team | Prize | Project Details | Team Members |
|-------|-------|------|-------|-----------------|--------------|
| Butterworth | 1st | B.A.T. (Bluetooth Assisted Tracking) | $5,000 | Indoor and outdoor visitor-pattern tracking — origin, destination, travel time, dwell time, paths — over a given period of time. Targeted museums and galleries assessing temporary and permanent exhibit impact and resource allocation. Significantly cheaper than Wi-Fi triangulation. | Jaime Enrique Duarte, Joan Aguilar Mayans, Robert Regue Grino, Ana Papio Toda, Estel Carbó Vinaixa, Yue Yu |
| Butterworth | 2nd | TasteBuds | $3,500 | A mobile application delivering personalized restaurant recommendations based on time of day and the user's mood/activity, with gaming elements for food discovery. Initial focus: UC Irvine students. | Nithin Jilla, Michael Thomas, Candace Wu, Derek Wong, Norman Tran, Bryan Cuevas |
| Butterworth | 3rd | Life Buoy | $1,500 | An iOS and web application wirelessly connecting patients to physicians, enabling video consultations, secure health records, e-prescriptions, and emergency relief capabilities with GPS-located medical centers. | Matthew Chan, Jacob Heller, Bryan Lam, Lita Patel, Peggy Bui, Joe Hanson |

## Notes

- B.A.T. later expanded its branding to **Beacon-Assisted Technologies** and produced a museum app called **Kaleri** (Greek for "gallery") that piloted at the Huntington Beach Art Center in early December 2013 with 40 participants exploring a contemporary art sculpture exhibit titled "Reverberation." The app used indoor positioning to identify nearby artworks and allowed visitors to rate, bookmark, and share pieces. Source: [engineering.uci.edu, January 2014](https://engineering.uci.edu/news/2014/1/engineering-students-test-their-app-local-art-center).
- B.A.T. was a notably interdisciplinary team: Joan Aguilar (mechanical engineering doctoral student, robotics), Jaime Duarte (mechanical engineering doctoral student, optimal control / prototyping), Robert Regue (transportation engineering doctoral student, team founder), Anna Papio (sensor networks), Yue Yu (information and computer science doctoral student), and Estel Carbó (UCI Extension certificate graduate, marketing communications).
- 2013 was the **last Butterworth-only year**. The 2014 cycle introduced the joint Beall Student Design Competition, raised the prize tier, and added a hardware track.
