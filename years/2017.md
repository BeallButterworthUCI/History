# 2017 UCI Beall Butterworth Competition

[← Back to main archive](../README.md)

**Theme:** Vertical Social Apps and Affordable Prosthetics.
**Prize tier:** $7,500 / $5,000 / $2,500 (last year at the original Beall prize tier).

## Awards

| Track | Place | Team | Prize | Project Details | Team Members |
|-------|-------|------|-------|-----------------|--------------|
| Butterworth | 1st | Memorlane | $7,500 | A collaborative photo-sharing app that streamlines categorization by allowing users to upload photos to communal albums tied to specific locations. | Evan Schein, Yocelyn Recinos, Michael Tran |
| Butterworth | 2nd | AirNotes | $5,000 | A wearable hand computer with haptic feedback ("Motion Analytic System / AirTouch") for note-taking and gesture input. | Olaoluwa Adesanya, Laura Beken, Brandon Cabrera |
| Butterworth | 3rd | Pippy | $2,500 | An AI-powered "on-demand anything" task-fulfillment marketplace. | Karan Kanwar, Douglas Huynh, Kausthub Raj Jadhav, Martin Gomez, Antonio Lopez, Sam Chen |
| Beall | 1st | Krobotech | $7,500 | An affordable competitive prosthetic hand designed to be price-comparable to consumer electronics. | Ethan Kirkley, Zepyour Khechadoorian, Cameron Hunt, Kevin Wong |
| Beall | 2nd | Red Lion Robotics | $5,000 | Low-cost motivational walking-rehab tools for stroke and orthopedic recovery. (Same core team as the 2016 Beall 2nd-place Red Lion Robotics, with Hwan Heo replacing Brandon Tsuge.) | Yasemin Sarigul-Klijn, Peter Wang, Hwan Heo |
| Beall | 3rd | Syntr Health Technologies | $2,500 | A point-of-care stem-cell therapy device for diabetic foot ulcers. | Ahmed Zobi, Hugo Salas, Justin Stovner, Jade Tassey, Adrian Bahani |

## Notes

- **Red Lion Robotics** placed 2nd in the Beall track in both 2016 and 2017 — the only confirmed back-to-back placement by the same team in the joint era (the 2017 roster swaps in Hwan Heo for Brandon Tsuge but retains the core).
- **Adrian Bahani** of Syntr Health (2017) returned in 2019 with the Beall 3rd-place HemaDynamics team.
- Source: [bbcomp.tech.uci.edu/past/2017-participants-and-results/](https://bbcomp.tech.uci.edu/past/2017-participants-and-results/).
