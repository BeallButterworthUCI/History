# 2017 UCI Beall Butterworth Competition

[← Back to main archive](../README.md)

**Theme:** Vertical Social Apps and Affordable Prosthetics.
**Prize tier:** $7,500 / $5,000 / $2,500 (last year at the original Beall prize tier).

## Awards

| Track | Place | Team | Prize | Project Details | Team Members |
|-------|-------|------|-------|-----------------|--------------|
| Butterworth | 1st | Memorlane | $7,500 | A collaborative photo-sharing iOS app that streamlines categorization by allowing users to upload photos to communal albums tied to specific locations. Same team won the 2017 Stella Zhang NVC Consumer Services 1st place in the same year - the **first same-year BBComp + NVC double win on record**, later joined by Sentinel (Butterworth 1st + NVC Grand Prize, 2024), Two Cube (Butterworth 2nd + NVC Life Sciences 1st, 2024), NeoMold (Beall 1st + NVC Runner-Up, 2024), and NasoCoustics (Beall 1st + NVC Life Sciences 1st, 2025) - **five documented same-year double wins in total**. **Evan Schein** (UCI BS in Biomedical Engineering; previously at UCI ANTrepreneur Center and UCI Heart Disease Prevention Program) remains the **listed Founder of Memorlane**. **Yocelyn Recinos** (UCI BS Biological Sciences) went on to a **Columbia University PhD program** in Systems Biology as an **NSF Graduate Research Fellow** and is now a **Patent Agent at Clark+Elbing** (Boston). | Evan Schein, Yocelyn Recinos, Michael Tran |
| Butterworth | 2nd | AirNotes | $5,000 | A wearable hand computer with haptic feedback ("Motion Analytic System / AirTouch") for note-taking and gesture input - originally an interactive glove for visually impaired students learning piano/woodwind. **Now operating as the active Pacific Northwest startup [Palmplug](https://www.newtechnorthwest.com/new-tech-founder-spotlight-olaoluwa-adesanya-founder-ceo-palmplug/), focused on Spatial Computing, Wearables, Health, Robotics, and AI.** Olaoluwa Adesanya is Founder & CEO; also at Meta. | Olaoluwa Adesanya, Laura Beken, Brandon Cabrera |

Co-founders Karan Kanwar and Martin Gomez later returned in 2018 with new team **Wing** (tech for corporate sustainable competitiveness) and won the 2018 Stella Zhang NVC Consumer Services 1st place - $10,000. **Wing was incorporated in 2018 as Wing AI Technologies (commonly Wing Assistant), a cloud-based contract-workforce platform for virtual assistants powered by AI plus real people. Karan Kanwar grew the company from his college dorm room to over 1,000 employees in 8 countries; the company went through UC Berkeley's SkyDeck Spring 2020 cohort and raised institutional VC from Berkeley SkyDeck and Troy Labs.** **Kausthub Raj Jadhav** (also a multi-year BBComp founder across 2016 FASHENDS and 2019 Float Robotics) is now Product Engineering Manager at LILA Games and co-founded SoCal's largest AI non-profit, AI@UCI. | Karan Kanwar, Douglas Huynh, Kausthub Raj Jadhav, Martin Gomez, Antonio Lopez, Sam Chen |

Reinkensmeyer** (UCI Anatomy & Neurobiology). Team lead Ethan Kirkley is now Mechanical Engineer II / R&D Engineering Manager at Thermo Fisher Scientific. | Ethan Kirkley, Zepyour Khechadoorian, Cameron Hunt, Kevin Wong |
| Beall | 2nd | Red Lion Robotics | $5,000 | Low-cost motivational walking-rehab tools for stroke and orthopedic recovery. (Same core team as the 2016 Beall 2nd-place Red Lion Robotics, with Hwan Heo replacing Brandon Tsuge.) | Yasemin Sarigul-Klijn, Peter Wang, Hwan Heo |
| Beall | 3rd | Syntr Health Technologies | $2,500 | A point-of-care stem-cell therapy device for diabetic foot ulcers. **Now an active commercial company at [syntrhealth.com](https://syntrhealth.com/) - $10M raise announced March 2023 + $2.2M seed (Aug 2021) + $300K Phase I SBIR/NIH grant (Oct 2017). Headquartered at University Lab Partners (UCI Research Park). Product: Syntrfuge automated fat-processing system for facial fat loss, knee osteoarthritis, diabetic foot ulcers.** Ahmed Zobi is CEO and Co-Founder. | Ahmed Zobi, Hugo Salas, Justin Stovner, Jade Tassey, Adrian Bahani |

## Notes

- **Red Lion Robotics** placed 2nd in the Beall track in both 2016 and 2017 - the only confirmed back-to-back placement by the same team in the joint era (the 2017 roster swaps in Hwan Heo for Brandon Tsuge but retains the core).
- **Adrian Bahani** of Syntr Health (2017) returned in 2019 with the Beall 3rd-place HemaDynamics team.
- **Krobotech** finalist in 2016 → 1st place in 2017 - a complete trajectory from "almost there" to winning team.
- **Memorlane** finalist in 2016 → 1st place in 2017 - same pattern as Krobotech, on the Butterworth side.
- **Ahmad Razavi & Nazanin Ghasemian** entered three teams across 2016 (Anti-Acne, Pick a Jacket or Not) and 2017 (Driving License, UCI Parking Lot) - among the most prolific multi-entry duos in the archive.
