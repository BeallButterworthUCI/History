# 2026 UCI Beall Butterworth Competition

[← Back to main archive](../README.md)

**Theme:** Agentic AI, Embodied Robotics, and Maternal-Neonatal Devices.
**Edition:** 22nd annual Butterworth, 13th annual Beall.
**Demo Day & Awards Ceremony:** **May 15, 2026, 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM** at **The Cove @ UCI Beall Applied Innovation** (5270 California Ave, Irvine, CA 92617) - described in the ICS press release as "a 31,000-square-foot collaborative work and event space located at University Research Park."

The 2026 cycle ran the standard six-month arc - fall 2025 kickoff, winter design workshops, spring product specifications, midpoint orals, and coaching sessions - with submissions closing on May 6, 2026 (extended from May 4) and Demo Day on May 15, 2026. Results were announced publicly on May 29, 2026.

## Awards

## Confirmed structure

- Two competitive tracks: **Butterworth Product Development** (Donald Bren School of ICS) and **Beall Student Design** (Henry Samueli School of Engineering).
- An **International Collaboration Prize** continuing the partnership with Pontifical Catholic University of Rio Grande do Sul (PUCRS), Brazil - the fourth consecutive year of the UCI / PUCRS partnership.
- Standard placement prizes: $10,000 (1st) / $6,500 (2nd) / $3,500 (3rd) per track.
- Director: David Ochi (adjunct professor, Donald Bren School of ICS and Paul Merage School of Business).

## Notes

- **Awards were presented by BBComp director David Ochi alongside interim Engineering Dean Faryar Jabbari and ICS Dean Marios Papaefthymiou** - corroborating the leadership transition documented elsewhere in this archive (Jabbari became interim Dean of the Henry Samueli School of Engineering effective March 1, 2026 after Dean Magnus Egerstedt departed for UNC Chapel Hill).
- The **International Collaboration Prize** (won by UNI) is listed on the official bbcomp.tech.uci.edu winners page **without a disclosed dollar amount**, consistent with prior years (2023 N3.AI, 2024 Cartello, 2025 Oslo). It is recorded here as `Not Listed`.
- **Maternal-neonatal health hardware** was a strong 2026 thread on the Beall side: **Neovi Medical / NeoFusion** (mechanical neonatal syringe pump) took 1st and **Natavue** (labor-monitoring catheter) took 2nd.
- **Agentic AI** ran through the Butterworth and International tracks: **BillBack** (autonomous billing-audit workflow), **Auréa** (generative-AI video production), and **UNI** (AI-agent companion).
- Socratic OC's roster lists two members who share the surname Kuchi (Bruhati Aarushi Kuchi and Rutu Aarabhi Kuchi).
- 2026 marks the **fourth consecutive year** of the UCI / PUCRS (Brazil) partnership for the International Collaboration Prize.
- Joint kickoff event with the Stella Zhang New Venture Competition was held on **October 22, 2025** at the Paul Merage School. The **Stella Zhang NVC Grand Finale + Awards** was scheduled for **May 21, 2026**, six days after the BBComp Demo Day.
- Sources: [ics.uci.edu, May 29, 2026](https://ics.uci.edu/2026/05/29/students-showcase-innovation-at-2026-beall-butterworth-competition/), [bbcomp.tech.uci.edu](https://bbcomp.tech.uci.edu/), [bbcomp.tech.uci.edu/schedule/](https://bbcomp.tech.uci.edu/schedule/).
