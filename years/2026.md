# 2026 UCI Beall Butterworth Competition (In Progress)

[← Back to main archive](../README.md)

**Edition:** 22nd annual Butterworth, 13th annual Beall.
**Demo Day & Awards Ceremony:** **May 15, 2026, 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM** at **The Cove @ UCI Beall Applied Innovation** (5270 California Ave, Irvine, CA 92617).

The 2026 cycle is in its final week as of this archive's last update. The fall 2025 kickoff, winter design workshops, spring product specifications, midpoint orals, and coaching sessions have all been completed; submissions closed on May 6, 2026 (extended from May 4).

## Confirmed structure

- Two competitive tracks: **Butterworth Product Development** (Donald Bren School of ICS) and **Beall Student Design** (Henry Samueli School of Engineering).
- An **International Collaboration Prize** continuing the partnership with Pontifical Catholic University of Rio Grande do Sul (PUCRS), Brazil.
- Standard placement prizes: $10,000 (1st) / $6,500 (2nd) / $3,500 (3rd) per track.
- Director: David Ochi (adjunct professor, Donald Bren School of ICS and Paul Merage School of Business).
