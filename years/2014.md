# 2014 UCI Beall Butterworth Competition (Inaugural Joint Edition)

[← Back to main archive](../README.md)

**Theme:** Bluetooth-Era Networking and Bedside Diagnostics.
**Edition:** 11th annual Butterworth + 1st annual Beall.
**Combined prize pool:** $30,000.
**Significance:** the first joint Beall-Butterworth awards ceremony, marking the structural unification of the ICS Butterworth Product Development Competition and the new Henry Samueli School of Engineering Beall Student Design Competition.

## Awards

| Track | Place | Team | Prize | Project Details | Team Members |
|-------|-------|------|-------|-----------------|--------------|
| Butterworth | 1st | Blueberry (later Sparky) | $7,500 | A passive networking app that creates a 10-foot Bluetooth bubble around the user. When two bubbles encounter each other, a silent connection logs metadata (time, location, duration) - automating "who you met" at conferences and social events. | Karan Sekhri, Adrien Deguzman, Sheng Xia, Tai Cao, Derek Omuro |
| Butterworth | 2nd | BeatPool | $5,000 | A music-pooling app that lets multiple users send songs from their personal libraries to a single shared speaker - a precursor to collaborative-playlist features now common on streaming platforms. **Cesar Ghali and Fulya Ozcan formally incorporated BeatPool as a company in 2015** (Irvine-based; party-playlist app where nearby friends can connect, add tracks, upvote and downvote in real time). The company did not raise institutional funding and is **listed as deadpooled on Tracxn as of 2025**. Cesar Ghali went on to complete a UCI CS PhD on content-centric networking security and is now a Software Engineer at Google. | Ekin Oguz, Sky Faber, Cesar Ghali, Fulya Ozcan |
| Butterworth | 3rd | Ant-mApp | $2,500 | A UCI campus-event hub mobile and web app for discovering on-campus activities. | Alexsandra Guerra, Esosa Agbonwaneten, Yerlan Turekeshov, Avedis Simonian, Maharshi Patel |
| Beall | 1st | Spero Diagnostics | $7,500 | A rapid sepsis diagnostic device targeting under three hours and under $100 per test, addressing the deadly prevalence of sepsis in hospital settings. | Jessica Motherwell, Jose Gallegos, Ernesto Sosa, Ting-Yi Chu, Maka Pennell, Zachary Campagna |
| Beall | 2nd | A Hundred Tiny Hands | $5,000 | Three "Inventor's Toolboxes" designed to teach children core engineering and science concepts. **Now an active academic nonprofit co-op at [100tinyhands.org](http://100tinyhands.org/)** with Kickstarter-funded products and a published children's book series ("EUREKA! ## Notes

- The 2014 cycle was the **inaugural year of the Beall Student Design Competition**, sponsored by Donald R. Beall and the Beall Family Foundation, and the **11th year of the Butterworth Product Development Competition**, originally sponsored by Paul Butterworth in the early 2000s.
- The 2014 source is the canonical Henry Samueli School of Engineering press release: [engineering.uci.edu, July 2014](https://engineering.uci.edu/news/2014/7/butterworth-and-beall-competitions-reward-best-both-hardware-and-software). The bbcomp.tech.uci.edu archive's 2014 page renders duplicated 2015 content (see [years/2015.md](2015.md) for the disambiguation note).
- David Ochi has continued as competition director through the present (2025); his "12+ years" tenure cited in 2024 places him with the program from at least the inaugural joint ceremony.
- 2014 raised the prize tier from the original Butterworth-era $5,000 / $3,500 / $1,500 (used 2012-2013) to $7,500 / $5,000 / $2,500. That tier persisted through 2017 before the 2018 raise to $10,000 / $6,500 / $3,500.
