# 2010 UCI Butterworth Product Development Competition

[← Back to main archive](../README.md)

**Track:** Butterworth-only.
**Edition:** 7th annual Butterworth Product Development Competition.
**Status:** **No public record found** of placements, team names, or members.

The Butterworth Product Development Competition was running in 2010 — by the 2014 framing the competition was already "11th annual," and a 2012 judge quote referenced "nine years" of competitors, both of which place 2010 as the 7th annual cycle. However, the legacy ICS news archive at `archive-www.ics.uci.edu/community/news/view_news?id=…` does not surface a 2010 results article in any current public form, and Google's index does not return one either.

## What we know

- The competition existed (per the 7th-annual / "since 2003–2004" arithmetic).
- Paul Butterworth was already the named sponsor (confirmed by 2012 references).
- The likely prize tier was the same $5,000 / $3,500 / $1,500 structure used in the verified 2012 and 2013 cycles, but this is not directly confirmed for 2010.

## What we are missing

- Team names and placements (1st, 2nd, 3rd).
- Team members.
- Project descriptions.
- Mentors and judges.
- The exact ceremony date.
- Whether the competition was held in Bren Hall (as in 2012) or elsewhere.

## How to help

If you participated in, judged, mentored, or attended the 2010 Butterworth Product Development Competition — or if you have a printed program booklet, a photo from the awards ceremony, or any contemporaneous email or press release — please open an issue using the [missing-year template](../.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/02-missing-year.md), or email it directly. We will fully credit any source.
