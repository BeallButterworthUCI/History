# 2021 UCI Beall Butterworth Competition

[← Back to main archive](../README.md)

**Theme:** At-Home Diagnostics and Youth-Led Fintech.
**Format:** held virtually due to COVID-19.

## Notes

- **Caitlyn Yang** of Grasshopper Fund was a Blackstone LaunchPad / Techstars Top 30 semi-finalist (out of 900) in 2020/2021, joined Wayfinder, founded the related venture StartSmart ([startsmart.co](https://startsmart.co/founding-story/)), and is **now Mechanical Engineer at SpaceX**. **StartSmart's framing problem**: per Yang, 69% of teenagers have a business idea but only 5% start a real, sustainable business; young entrepreneurs are often dismissed for lack of experience and have no debt-free capital. Site: [grasshopperfund.com](https://www.grasshopperfund.com/). Source: [innovation.uci.edu](https://innovation.uci.edu/news/caitlyn-yang-sends-young-entrepreneurs-soaring/).
- **Lily McGrale** of forMED was a 2021 Blackstone LaunchPad Fellow and a Summer 2021 VentureWell E-Team participant; the team also won the 2022 Stella Zhang life-sciences track and was a finalist for the 2022 TigerLaunch Competition. **McGrale is now an R&D Engineer II at Medtronic** - the TonoLase product does not appear to have reached commercialization as a standalone company.
- **Aryan Agarwal** (forMED) is now **R&D Engineer at Acorai** - Swedish heart-failure monitoring startup that closed €4.1M seed in January 2024 and earned FDA Breakthrough Device Designation in late 2024. **Ruben Shakya** (forMED) is now R&D/Quality Engineer II at Edwards Lifesciences. **Raphael Francis Aguas** (forMED + AllerChain) is now an **NSF Graduate Research Fellow + UCI BME PhD student** focused on ocular devices and vision science; previously researched SARS-CoV-2 electrochemical sensors in Khine's lab. The forMED alumni network has placed firmly in the medical-device industry and academia.
- **forMED's precursor team:** the 2019 BBComp combined-track finalist **AllerChain** (Lily McGrale, Aryan Agarwal, Ruben Shakya, Raphael Francis Aguas, Kamalesh Ananthakrishnan, Peter Jang, Jehan Kobe Chang) is the same core team that returned in 2021 as forMED with a new product - one of the clearest "finalist → winner with new pitch" trajectories in the archive.
- **The Blue Box (Diego Esparza, Lauren Hoshiko)** has a possible commercial successor in **The Blue Box Biomedical Solutions, S.L.** ([thebluebox.ai](https://thebluebox.ai/)), founded by UCI Master of Embedded Cyber-physical Systems graduates Judit Giró Benet (CEO) and Billy Chen. The product (at-home breast-cancer detection inspired by dogs' olfactory systems) matches the 2021 BBComp pitch closely. The exact relationship between the BBComp 2021 team and the Spanish operating company is **unverified** and could be (a) the same project, (b) a successor built on shared UCI research, or (c) coincident concept naming. The operating company has reached the TOP 9 of the EIT Health & Roche Accelerator 2024 and Judit Giró Benet won the 2020 James Dyson Award.
- The 2021 cycle's all-virtual format reduced the public press footprint compared to in-person years; per-placement prize amounts were not publicly enumerated.
- Source: [bbcomp.tech.uci.edu/past/past-2021/](https://bbcomp.tech.uci.edu/past/past-2021/).
