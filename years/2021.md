# 2021 UCI Beall Butterworth Competition

[← Back to main archive](../README.md)

**Theme:** At-Home Diagnostics and Youth-Led Fintech.
**Format:** held virtually due to COVID-19.

## Awards

| Track | Place | Team | Prize | Project Details | Team Members |
|-------|-------|------|-------|-----------------|--------------|
| Butterworth | 1st | Grasshopper Fund | Not Listed | A startup marketplace and funding platform for youth-led startups, providing online entrepreneurship coaching, a network of mentors and peers, and the first rewards-based crowdfunding platform investing in youth-led startups. Joined the Wayfinder incubator post-competition. | Caitlyn Yang, Ovya Barani, Nathan Tisuela |
| Butterworth | 2nd | Nutripair | Not Listed | A personalized nutrition-pairing platform. | Bing Mo, Catlin Tran |
| Butterworth | 3rd | Armory | Not Listed | An interactive cybersecurity-learning platform with hands-on labs. | Kumar Vaibhav, Maaz Syed Adeeb, Vineet Bharot, Shuvam Ghosh |
| Beall | 1st | forMED Technologies (TonoLase) | Not Listed | An eye-care startup improving glaucoma management via the TonoLase, an at-home non-invasive intraocular-pressure monitoring device. | Lily McGrale, Aryan Agarwal, Ruben Shakya, Raphael Francis Aguas, Kamalesh Ananthakrishnan |
| Beall | 2nd | Phoraptor | Not Listed | An affordable phoropter for low-income communities that lack access to traditional bulky and expensive vision-test devices. | Rahul Sreedasyam, Ishaan Sharma, Soham Patel, Shaan Braich, Alon Pavlov, Abhishek Kulkarni |
| Beall | 3rd | The Blue Box | Not Listed | An AI-powered biomedical device modeled on a dog's olfactory system, aimed at non-invasive in-home breast-cancer testing. | Diego Esparza, Lauren Hoshiko |

## Notes

- **Caitlyn Yang** of Grasshopper Fund went on to be a Blackstone LaunchPad / Techstars Top 30 semi-finalist (out of 900) in 2020/2021, joined Wayfinder, and later founded the related venture StartSmart. Site: [grasshopperfund.com](https://www.grasshopperfund.com/), [startsmart.co](https://startsmart.co/founding-story/). Source: [innovation.uci.edu](https://innovation.uci.edu/news/caitlyn-yang-sends-young-entrepreneurs-soaring/).
- **Lily McGrale** of forMED was a 2021 Blackstone LaunchPad Fellow and a Summer 2021 VentureWell E-Team participant; the team also won the 2022 Stella Zhang life-sciences track. McGrale is now an R&D Engineer II at Medtronic.
- The 2021 cycle's all-virtual format reduced the public press footprint compared to in-person years; per-placement prize amounts were not publicly enumerated.
- Source: [bbcomp.tech.uci.edu/past/past-2021/](https://bbcomp.tech.uci.edu/past/past-2021/).
