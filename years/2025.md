## Notes

- Kanu Chandra previously won the 2022 Butterworth 1st place with Leprendo and is the most repeat-named UCI competitor across the years documented in this archive.
- Kimia Yazdani previously placed 2nd in the 2023 Butterworth track with Nibble.
- NasoCoustics maintains a project page at [nasocoustics.uci.design](http://www.nasocoustics.uci.design/) and projects a $330M nasometry market opportunity by 2030 in their pitch materials.
- The Oslo team appears in the Brazilian Collaboration / International Collaboration Prize lineage that was launched in 2023; the prize was rebranded from "Brazilian" to "International" framing in 2025.
