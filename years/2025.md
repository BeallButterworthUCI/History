# 2025 UCI Beall Butterworth Competition

[← Back to main archive](../README.md)

**Theme:** AI-Native Healthcare and Specialized Medical Devices.

## Awards

| Track | Place | Team | Prize | Project Details | Team Members |
|-------|-------|------|-------|-----------------|--------------|
| Butterworth | 1st | Florence | $10,000 | Intelligent real-time patient monitoring and alert system that empowers hospitals and medical practitioners worldwide by monitoring patients 24/7 through analyzing live video streams and predicting incoming serious events. Uses a **custom fine-tuned Vision Large Language Model**, real-time **human-pose segmentation**, and live vital monitoring to dramatically reduce medical practitioner fatigue in high-patient-volume settings. **Cross-disciplinary team**: Andrew Cai (now at Virio), Audrey Lu, Nathan Che (now Software Engineer at TwelveLabs; previously Blackstone, Microsoft, ROBLOX) from ICS; Yurina Tamura from the Paul Merage School of Business (BBA 2023-2026). | Andrew Cai, Audrey Lu, Nathan Che, Yurina Tamura |
| Butterworth | 2nd | AINGELX | $6,500 | A personalized advisor that matches SaaS startups to investors based on due-diligence-level data, making the screening process more efficient. **Mehrnaz Motamed** is a PhD student at UCI Bren ICS specializing in **deep generative models and time series**; previously Data Scientist and AI Intern at Endura Technologies (deep neural networks for high-performance power delivery) and Software Engineer at Ubtech Robotics (computer vision R&D). **Kimia Yazdani** is a UCI Graduate Student Researcher doing CS research on biological networks and astrophysics with **Dr. Wayne Hayes**; ORCID 0009-0009-2922-9945; co-author of the 2024 Bioinformatics paper "New GO-based measures in multiple network alignment" (Oxford Academic). **Asal Askari** is a PhD candidate in UCI's Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering; research on low-impact-development technologies for urban stream syndrome ("From Rain Tanks to Catchments"); expertise in catchment hydrology, runoff, and green infrastructure. | Asal Askari, Kimia Yazdani, Mehrnaz Motamed |
| Butterworth | 3rd | Vision Board | $3,500 | A technology that merges the worlds of board games and video games to create a new immersive sensory experience. The Vision Board demo blends a physical Tic-Tac-Toe board with real-time 3D graphics and audio. **Vision Board also placed 1st in the storytelling track for $10K** (so the team's combined Butterworth-cycle take was $13,500). **Derek Yazan is Founder and Lead Experience Designer** (CS major at UCI Bren School of ICS); active in the **Themed Entertainment Association at UCI**, the **ANTrepreneur Center AI Innovation Challenge**, and **Beall Applied Innovation's NSF I-Corps program**. **Lukas Hauge** is a member of the **UCI Video Game Development Club**. The team brought Vision Board to UCI's Z3 convention. | Derek Yazan, Ethan Frias, Lukas Hauge, Phillip Peterson |
| Beall | 1st | NasoCoustics | $10,000 | A portable, wireless, child-friendly nasometry device for clinicians to measure hypernasality in children with cleft palate, post-surgery. Hypernasality affects 28.5-40.5% of children with cleft palate. **Also won the 2025 Stella Zhang NVC Life Sciences 1st place** - same year double win. **Neha Muvvala** is President of BMES at UCI; **also on the winning Venaptic team in the 2024 UCI Cardiovascular Device Pitch ($20K)**. | Dave Soliven, Jordan Ulibarri, Kenji Moses Olaso Inducta, Natalie Benneian, Neha Muvvala, Neharika Gopikrishnan |
| Beall | 2nd | VeinGuard | $6,500 | An emergency cervical collar that protects the cervical spine without compressing the jugular veins, addressing a known failure mode of standard collars. Priced at $50/unit with an 80% gross profit margin. **Medical advisors: Dr. Yama Akbari (UCI Neurology) + Dr. Christine King (UCI BME).** Multi-channel B2B + DTC + licensing business model. Patented side-opening cervical collar design eliminates lateral pressure (stabilizes head via mandible/chest/occiput, preserving full venous return). **Submitted to the 2025 James Dyson Award.** **Bora Keresteci** is a UCI BME undergraduate (2021-2025) and Data Science Intern at Vena Vitals (the 2019 Qardiac follow-on company); previously at BEAMS Lab, UCI Cardiovascular Innovation and Research Center, Aureka Biotechnologies, and TRUE Lab. | Bora Keresteci, Erfan Khoshnevis, Rohan Kunduru, Sujal Bobba, Varu Vummidi, Raveena Atwal, Marlena Ramanis |
| Beall | 3rd | REACH | $3,500 | An ML-powered electrotherapy device for stroke survivors that uses surface EMG plus functional electrical stimulation to support activities of daily living in a home setting. **Tim Oh** completed UCI CSE and is now pursuing a **PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering at UCLA**. | Andrew Eck, Edmund Totah, HyungCheol Kim, Michael Song, Nathan Yang, Tim Oh |
| International Collaboration | 1st | Oslo (Birdhouse by Oslo) | Not Listed | Passive, contact-free camera that monitors seniors' vitals using **thermal and optical sensors**, alerting caregivers to early health issues - no wearables or interaction needed. **Kanu Chandra** is now at **V11**; previously held positions at Soma Capital, Brainbase (YC W24), Manifest, Leprendo, Commit the Change, and the UCI Engineering Student Council. | UCI: Alison Iversen, Jose Callejas, Joseph Marquez, Kanu Chandra. PUCRS: Ana Carolina Palhares Poletto, Bárbara da Silva Dapper, Luiza Dal Mas Pasini |

- Kimia Yazdani previously placed 2nd in the 2023 Butterworth track with Nibble.
- NasoCoustics maintains a project page at [nasocoustics.uci.design](http://www.nasocoustics.uci.design/) and projects a $330M nasometry market opportunity by 2030 in their pitch materials.
