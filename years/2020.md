# 2020 UCI Beall Butterworth Competition (Cancelled)

[← Back to main archive](../README.md)

**Status:** Cancelled.
**Reason:** Global COVID-19 pandemic and UCI campus closures (effective March 2020).

The 2020 cycle was cancelled in its traditional format, with no awards ceremony, no Demo Day, and no published placements. Planning for the following year pivoted to a virtual format, and the competition returned in **2021** as a fully virtual edition.

## Notes

- Confirmation of the 2020 cancellation: [ics.uci.edu, June 2022](https://ics.uci.edu/2022/06/23/2022-butterworth-product-development-competition-back-in-full-swing/) — the article explicitly references the 2020 cancellation and the 2021 pivot.
- Some teams that had begun work on 2020 entries continued and competed in the 2021 virtual cycle, though no public reconciliation exists between the two cohorts.
- This is the only documented cancellation in the joint Beall–Butterworth era (2014–present).
