# 2023 UCI Beall Butterworth Competition

[← Back to main archive](../README.md)

**Theme:** Wearable Health Sensors and Maternal-Health AI.
**Edition:** 9th annual joint Beall + Butterworth.
**Ceremony:** May 24, 2023 at the Calit2 Auditorium.
**Scale:** 13 Beall teams + 16 Butterworth teams + 17 international (UCI / PUCRS) teams = 46 total competing teams.
**Notable:** the 13 Beall teams scored within three points of each other (out of 100), an unprecedented level of competitive density per the engineering.uci.edu writeup.
**Notable first:** inaugural year of the UCI / PUCRS (Pontifical Catholic University of Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil) partnership, with 33 PUCRS students paired with 40+ UCI students across 17 international teams.

## Notes

- Eupnea went on to win the **Microsoft Imagine Cup Epic Challenge 2023 American Region** ($5K plus a finalist slot for the $100K grand prize) and reached the **Top 3 worldwide** at the **Imagine Cup World Championship 2023** (TAWI from Kenya won the championship). The full Imagine Cup roster includes a fourth UCI graduate teammate, **Yogita Mallikarjun Pattan**, plus **Aditya Sreerama** from Duke - both of whom were not listed on the BBComp results page but appear on the Microsoft Imagine Cup team page and the [grad.uci.edu announcement](https://grad.uci.edu/2023/01/18/narke-iyyhappan-pattan-win-microsoft-imagine-cup-epic-challenge/). Co-founder Chidroop Iyyhappan is now an Engineer at Meta.
- The Brazilian Collaboration Prize was a new award category in 2023, facilitated by PUCRS associate professor and UCI visiting scholar Rafael Prikladnicki. The winning team gets a travel award for an exchange visit at the partner university.
- Dean Magnus Egerstedt described the year as "truly a celebration of what we can dream up when we get together. It is a roadmap for what we want to achieve moving forward." Source: [engineering.uci.edu, June 2023](https://engineering.uci.edu/news/2023/6/beall-butterworth-competition-results-announced).
- Judge Angela Gribble (President of Small Seeds Inc., judge since the inaugural year): "I enjoy the growing diversity and networking in the competition. The students are reaching other parts of UCI, the community and now internationally in Brazil."
- Pulsera teammate Nicolette Fulcomer on the prize: "We want to use the prize money to further develop our prototype and create a final version. Then we'll try to push it out to market and begin testing it. Hopefully, it can start helping people as soon as possible."
- WEngineers / Lengage Ware CEO Kristin Hagen on the future: "I want to continue working on the prototype until I'm ready to start a company."
- N3.AI teammate Nitish Nagesh: "We are excited to do more customer discovery and launch our product soon."
- Source for partnership announcement: [engineering.uci.edu, May 2023](https://engineering.uci.edu/news/2023/5/uci-partners-international-university-beall-butterworth-competition).
